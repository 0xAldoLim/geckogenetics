# Calculation assumptions

Reviewed 9 November 2026.

1. **The user supplies the evidence.** Entering a known genotype is an assertion based on records or an explicit scenario assumption. The app cannot verify an animal’s genotype. Educational presets are labeled as assumptions in their evidence notes.
2. **Diploid, autosomal Mendelian inheritance.** Each parent contributes one allele per modeled locus. Genotypes are represented by 0, 1, or 2 variant copies; notation is scoped to its trait, not a molecular allele identifier.
3. **Independent assortment is a conditional model assumption.** Joint distributions multiply locus distributions. This is not proof that every pair of loci has been physically mapped or shown to segregate independently. No linkage distances, recombination fractions, penetrance rates, or viability adjustments are invented. Different snow lines and insufficiently established eye-locus interactions are left unmodeled.
4. **Possible hets are distributions.** A non-visual possible het is a mixture of non-carrier and carrier genotypes, not a mixture including homozygous visual animals. The probability is supplied by the user. Parental carrier probabilities and probabilities across loci are assumed independent. Shared ancestry or correlated evidence may violate this assumption; the app does not infer these correlations.
5. **Scenario assumptions are explicit.** New pairings default to a standard estimate: an omitted entry at an active modeled locus is assumed to have zero variant copies. Each such parent/trait assumption is listed beside the results and in copied text. The option is saved and shared with the pairing. Turning it off leaves missing entries unknown and excludes unresolved loci. Explicitly unknown records remain unknown in either mode. Assumptions never mutate parental records. Old saved records/links without the option remain record-only. Loci omitted by both parents stay outside the calculation.
6. **Names do not prove hidden alleles.** Selecting a visual morph enters only the components specified by that selection. It cannot establish unrelated non-carrier states. A photo or normal appearance is not evidence of all hidden genes. Giant, Ghost, Pastel, and ambiguous snow/eye relationships are retained as reference labels rather than assigned speculative genotypes.
7. **Albino strains are separate.** Tremper, Bell, and Rainwater are modeled as distinct recessive loci, supported by complementation observations. A Tremper × Bell cross predicts all double het offspring when non-carrier states at the other strain are entered or explicitly assumed through estimate mode. Explicitly unknown cross-strain records remain unknown.
8. **Phenotype labels are expected expression at modeled loci.** Full Mendelian expression is assumed for those labels. Masking, variable expressivity, age, environment, epistasis, and interactions with unmodeled traits can alter appearance. A combination card lists alleles/expected expressions, not a validated prediction of the exact visible animal. No image is a generated hatchling photograph.
9. **Lineage is separate.** Polygenic/line-bred annotations are ancestry records with no offspring expression percentage. Family names remain prominent in result titles, badges and copied summaries. Lighter/darker or pattern examples are explicitly unweighted guides; they do not change genotype groups, probabilities, or parental records. A named line’s presence does not quantify color, ancestry proportion, health, or quality. No breeding dataset or color-intensity model is fabricated.
10. **Grouping preserves probabilities.** Appearance groups hold every underlying genotype and exact unconditional weight. Carrier information within a group is conditional on that group. Genotype cards describe hypothetical genetic outcomes; appearance alone cannot distinguish het and non-carrier hatchlings.
11. **Exact internal arithmetic.** Fractions use reduced BigInt numerators and denominators. Chart/card percentages are rounded only for display. Largest-remainder card labels sum to 100% across the complete outcome set; separately rounded subgroup percentages can differ by 0.01 percentage point. Rare outcomes may round to zero; expanded exact genotype probabilities remain in the engine.
12. **Per egg, not per clutch.** Percentages are statistical expectations for independent eggs, not a guarantee of any clutch composition.
13. **Breeder shorthand requires review.** Dotted h./ph., joined Snow names and documented compact combinations are read in source order. A confirmed het list can contain several recessives; qualifiers are not lost when multiple traits follow. T, R and ambiguous MSR/SSR forms require explicit interpretations. Missing probabilities stay unknown. Compound possible hets require separate component states and explicit independence confirmation before numeric marginal scenarios are used. Lineage wording never assigns visual/carrier alleles. Conflicting states are rejected. Unresolved labels are preserved.
14. **Observation is separate.** Comparison checks only recognized, modeled visual components against the entered model. Hidden-carrier claims, line-bred appearance, and unresolved labels are inconclusive. Compatibility is not proof of genotype or parentage. An apparent mismatch does not cause inference or mutation of parental states.
15. **Welfare limits.** Lemon Frost and Enigma labels and recognized risky combinations withhold predictions and discourage breeding. Warnings distinguish peer-reviewed Lemon Frost evidence from Enigma breeder reports. Reported NDBE and White & Yellow concerns are recorded without invented incidence. No health, sex, survival, fertility, or inbreeding probability is calculated.
16. **Input and performance bounds.** Pairing links and saved records are strictly schema-validated, versioned, and size-limited. The model has nine locus definitions. TUG Snow uses a conditional originator-reported dominant model, with no separate Super TUG appearance. Positive or unresolved Mack and TUG entries are excluded together rather than assumed independent. The eight mutually compatible variable loci retain at most 3^8 = 6,561 joint genotype outcomes. The chart groups overflow into “other outcomes”; cards load in batches of 12. The complete distribution is retained.
17. **Local storage limitations.** Storage can be disabled, full, cleared, or corrupted. Errors are shown; corrupt data is not silently overwritten. Share links contain entered notes. No remote storage or analytics is configured.

18. **Marketplace records are examples.** Product text and last-slide card fields are breeder claims. Card identifiers that differ from listing identifiers are flagged in the audit. Line: Tremper/Bell is context, not a carrier assertion. Unresolved albino strains cannot be treated as absent under the unlisted-gene option. Oreo/Zulu inputs are excluded from the leopard-gecko model. Exact color, unverified project inheritance and undocumented joint carrier probabilities remain unresolved.

19. **Faunary selections preserve qualifiers.** The dated source-label index comes from full public descriptions and titles, with each label interpreted by the shared resolver. Direct selection is offered only when its meaning is explicit and consistent; uncertain probabilities, strain, dosage, codes and conflicts remain in review. A confirmed visual component or confirmed carrier entry is not downgraded by a broader possible-combination claim. Unknown project codes assign no alleles, and bundles do not merge multiple animals into one genotype. MP is Murphy Patternless; h MP, ph MP and visual MP are distinct states.
